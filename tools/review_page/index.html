How to decide (counting rule B)

Each tile is one spot where the original label and the detector disagree. Decide what is really there.

  • Colony: a distinct colony with its own edge, at least 0.5 mm across (about 11 px on the photo).
  • Not: empty agar, haze, scratch, fibre, sticker text, rim glare, bubble, or a duplicate of a colony already marked.
  • Speck: a real pin-point dot 0.3–0.5 mm. Not counted, but the plate goes to review.
  • Unsure: you cannot tell. It is left out of training.

Ring colours on the crops and the plate photo:

  • labelled, but the detector did not find it
  • found by the detector, not labelled
  • very large (over 25 mm): one mould, a lawn, or a plate-wide box?
  • label and detector agree (photo only, no decision needed)

Keyboard: focus a tile (Tab or click), then C colony, N not, S speck, U unsure. The next tile is selected automatically. [ and ] change plate.

Click the plate photo to zoom. If a whole plate is a lawn or so overgrown that colonies cannot be told apart, mark it TNTC / overgrown and skip its tiles.
